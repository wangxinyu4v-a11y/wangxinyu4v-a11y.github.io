Research

Current directions

Topological mechanics

I investigate topological Maxwell lattices, with a focus on stress localization, soft modes, states of self stress, nonlinear deformation, symmetry reduction, and fracture.

Related paper: Topological fracture in Maxwell lattices

Kirigami-based nonlinear mechanics

I study how to use cutting patterns and stability analysis to achieve shape-morphing structures with functions.

Related papers: Interleaved kirigami · Helical bilayers

Soft & programmable materials

My work in liquid crystals, liquid crystal elastomers, and colloids explores how structure, anisotropy, and active or stimulus-driven processes can create programmable mechanical behavior.

Related papers: LCE shape morphing · Moiré liquid crystals · Nematic colloidal entanglement

Architected materials

I use geometry and mechanics to design structures with unusual deformation, deployment, instability, and damage response, supported by nonlinear finite element modeling.

Related papers: Interleaved kirigami · Deployable mesh reflectors
