March 2026

Invited talk at Geometers of Soft Materials

I gave an invited talk, “Topology-driven versatile design and collective dynamics in nematic liquid crystals,” at the Geometers of Soft Materials Symposium at the University of Pennsylvania, a workshop celebrating Randy Kamien’s 60th birthday.

This event was especially memorable for me. I spent two years at Penn as a visiting Ph.D. researcher, and that experience became a turning point in my path from engineering toward soft matter physics. Returning to Penn to share my own work made the symposium particularly meaningful. I will never forget my time there.
