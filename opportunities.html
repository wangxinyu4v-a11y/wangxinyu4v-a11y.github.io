Interested in working with me?

Students

I welcome students at all levels, including undergraduate, master's, and Ph.D. students. If you are interested in theoretical or computational research in soft matter, topological mechanics, liquid crystals, metamaterials, or architected materials, I have research projects for you.

You do not need to arrive with all of the necessary research skills. Projects can be scoped to your background, and methods such as finite element modeling, numerical computation, mechanics, symmetry, and topology can be learned through the research.

Interested students: Please email me with a brief introduction, your current level/program, your research interests, and a CV if available.

Postdoctoral researchers

I am also interested in working with postdoctoral researchers whose interests overlap with my research directions. Current postdoctoral opportunities and relevant fellowship routes will be posted here.
