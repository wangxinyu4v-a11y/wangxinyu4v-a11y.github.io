Opportunities

Interested in working with me?

I welcome inquiries from prospective students, postdoctoral fellows, and visiting researchers with backgrounds in physics, applied mathematics, mechanical engineering, materials science, and civil engineering. My research focuses on theoretical and computational approaches to soft and architected materials. Email me if you are interested!

Students at all levels, including undergraduate, master's, and PhD students, are welcome to get in touch. Projects can be scoped to your background, and methods such as finite element modeling, numerical computation, mechanics, symmetry, and topology can be learned through the research.
